Chewy Brown Butter Chocolate Chip Cookies

I have been chasing the perfect chocolate chip cookie for about ten years now, and I finally think I have found it. The secret is browning the butter first.
Advertisement
Why you'll love these cookies
Crispy edges with soft and chewy centers that stay soft for days.
Brown butter gives a deep toffee flavor that regular cookies just don't have.
Ingredients
Brown butter: Cook the butter until it smells nutty and the milk solids turn golden brown, then let it cool.
Brown sugar: Use dark brown sugar for an even chewier cookie if you have it on hand.
Chocolate: I like a mix of chips and a chopped bar so you get puddles of chocolate.
How to make brown butter chocolate chip cookies
Brown the butter and let it cool for 10 minutes before mixing.

Chewy Brown Butter Chocolate Chip Cookies

Print Recipe
Crispy edges, gooey centers and nutty brown butter in every bite.
Prep Time 15 minutes
Cook Time 11 minutes
Chill Time 30 minutes
Total Time 56 minutes
Servings 18 cookies
Ingredients
1x2x3x
▢ 1 cup (226g) unsalted butter
▢ 1 ¼ cups (250g) packed dark brown sugar
▢ ¼ cup (50g) granulated sugar
▢ 2 large eggs, room temperature
▢ 2 teaspoons pure vanilla extract
▢ 2 ¼ cups (281g) all-purpose flour
▢ 1 teaspoon baking soda
▢ 1 teaspoon cornstarch
▢ ½ teaspoon salt
▢ 1 ½ cups (270g) semi-sweet chocolate chips
▢ flaky sea salt, for topping (optional)
US Customary – Metric
Instructions
In a light-colored skillet, melt the butter over medium heat. Keep stirring until it foams and brown bits form on the bottom, about 5-8 minutes. Pour into a large bowl and let cool for 10 minutes.
Whisk the brown sugar and granulated sugar into the brown butter until smooth. Add the eggs and vanilla and whisk until glossy.
In a separate bowl, whisk together the flour, baking soda, cornstarch and salt. Fold into the wet ingredients, then fold in the chocolate chips.
Cover and chill the dough for at least 30 minutes.
Preheat oven to 350°F (177°C). Line baking sheets with parchment paper.
Scoop 3-tablespoon balls of dough onto the sheets and bake for 10-12 minutes until edges are golden. Sprinkle with flaky salt and cool on the pan for 5 minutes.
Notes
Dough can be chilled for up to 3 days.
For thicker cookies, chill the scooped dough balls overnight.
