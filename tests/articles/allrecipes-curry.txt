2 tablespoons vegetable oil
1 large onion, diced
3 cloves garlic, minced
1 tablespoon grated fresh ginger
2 tablespoons curry powder
1 teaspoon ground cumin
1 ½ pounds boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into bite-size pieces
1 (14 ounce) can coconut milk
1 (14.5 ounce) can diced tomatoes
1 teaspoon salt
2 tablespoons chopped fresh cilantro
Directions
Step 1
Heat oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add onion and cook until soft and translucent, about 5 minutes.
Dotdash Meredith Food Studios
Step 2
Stir in garlic, ginger, curry powder, and cumin; cook until fragrant, about 1 minute.
Step 3
Add chicken and cook, stirring, until no longer pink on the outside, about 5 minutes.
Step 4
Pour in coconut milk and tomatoes; season with salt. Simmer until chicken is cooked through and sauce has thickened, about 20 minutes.
Step 5
Garnish with cilantro and serve over rice.
