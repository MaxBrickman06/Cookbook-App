Fresh Mango Salsa

Bright, sweet and spicy. Great with chips or grilled fish.

2 ripe mangoes, diced
1/2 red onion, finely chopped
1 jalapeño, seeded and minced
1/4 cup chopped cilantro
Juice of 2 limes
Pinch of salt

Combine everything in a bowl and toss gently.
Let it sit for 15 minutes so the flavors come together.
Taste and add more lime or salt if needed.
