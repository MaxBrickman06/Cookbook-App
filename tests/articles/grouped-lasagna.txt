Weeknight Skillet Lasagna
All the flavor of lasagna in one pan and about half the time.
Yield: 6 servings
Ingredients
For the sauce:
1 tablespoon olive oil
1 pound Italian sausage, casings removed
1 small yellow onion, finely chopped
4 garlic cloves, minced
1 (28-ounce) can crushed tomatoes
1 teaspoon dried oregano
Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
For the cheese layer:
1 cup whole-milk ricotta
1 cup shredded mozzarella, divided
½ cup grated Parmesan, plus more for serving
8 ounces lasagna noodles, broken into pieces
Method
For the sauce:
1. Heat the oil in a large deep skillet over medium-high heat. Add the sausage and cook, breaking it up, until browned, about 6 minutes.
2. Add the onion and garlic and cook until soft, about 4 minutes. Stir in the tomatoes, oregano, 2 cups water, and season with salt and pepper.
Assemble:
3. Stir in the noodles, cover, and simmer, stirring occasionally, until the noodles are tender, 15 to 18 minutes.
4. Dollop the ricotta over the top and sprinkle with the mozzarella and Parmesan. Cover until melted, about 3 minutes. Serve hot.
