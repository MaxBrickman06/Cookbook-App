10 Minute Garlic Noodles

Ingredients

  • 8 oz spaghetti
  • 4 tbsp butter
  • 6 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar

Instructions

  1. Cook the spaghetti until al dente.
  2. Melt the butter and cook the garlic until golden.
  3. Stir in soy sauce and sugar, then toss with the noodles.
