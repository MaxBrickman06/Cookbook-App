3-ingredient banana oat cookies 🍌🍪 no flour, no sugar!

You need:
• 2 ripe bananas
• 1 cup rolled oats
• 1/3 cup chocolate chips

Mash the bananas. Stir in oats and chips. Scoop onto a lined tray and bake at 350°F for 15 minutes. Let cool and enjoy!
