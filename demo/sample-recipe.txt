Garlic Parmesan Roasted Broccoli
Jump to Recipe · Print · Pin

My kids actually ask for this one! It's the easiest side dish I know, and it only needs one pan.

Prep Time: 5 minutes
Cook Time: 20 minutes
Servings: 4

Ingredients
1 lb broccoli florets
2 tbsp olive oil
3 cloves garlic, minced
1/2 tsp salt
1/4 cup Parmesan cheese, grated
1 lemon

Instructions
1. Heat the oven to 425°F.
2. Toss the broccoli with the olive oil, garlic and salt on a baking sheet.
3. Roast for 18 to 20 minutes, until the edges are crispy.
4. Sprinkle with Parmesan and squeeze the lemon over the top.
