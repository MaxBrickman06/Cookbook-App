Also add the ability to create shopping lists that can be found in their own lists section. In every recipe, I want the ingredients not currently in the fridge to be highlighted red and shown at the top of the ingredients list before the rest of them. Also allow the user to turn off the fridge function for recipes so that ingredients arent filtered to the top and highlighted.
